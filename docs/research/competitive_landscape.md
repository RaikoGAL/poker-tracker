# Competitive Landscape: Poker Improvement & Tracking Tools (with emphasis on app-club players on ClubGG / PokerBros / PPPoker)

Research date: 2026-11-05. All prices are as observed on the cited page on the cited date; App Store prices are US unless noted. "App-club fit" = whether the product works for a mobile-first player on ClubGG/PokerBros/PPPoker who has no hand-history (HH) file export.

Methodology note: Reddit and TwoPlusTwo were not directly reachable via the search tooling used (searches returned app-store aggregators instead of forum threads). Review quotes below therefore come from App Store listings and App Store review aggregators (justuseapp, appfollow, appgoblin), and from trade-press reviews (PokerNews, Pokerfuse, PokerStrategy, VIP-Grinders, PokerListings). This is flagged as a gap at the end.

## KQ1. Category 1 — Bankroll/session trackers: leaders, pricing, core loop, praise, complaints, app-club fit

### Takeaway
The category is dominated by three long-lived indie iOS apps (Poker Bankroll Tracker by Pixelnomads, Pokerbase, Poker Analytics) at ~$20–35/yr, plus a long tail of 2025–2026 newcomers with near-zero installs. The core loop everywhere is manual session entry (buy-in / cash-out / duration) and every one of them works for app-club players only in the trivial sense that manual entry is platform-agnostic; none ingests anything from ClubGG/PokerBros automatically. Complaints cluster on subscription pricing, data loss/sync, and abandoned apps rather than on missing analysis.

### Inferences
- The incumbents have small but durable user bases (Poker Analytics Android ~21K installs / ~400 MAU; Pokerbase 187 US ratings; PBT 93 US ratings) — this is a niche measured in thousands of paying users per app, not hundreds of thousands.
- "Praise" is almost entirely about developer responsiveness and reliability, not about insight; "complaints" are about price-for-what-it-does and losing data. This suggests the job-to-be-done is a trustworthy ledger, and that analytics features above the ledger are not what converts.
- Every tracker is live-poker-first (venues, trips, tips, receipts, WSOP calendars). Online/app-club support is a bolt-on (Poker Analytics' "bankroll difference" entry mode from 2018; Pokerbase users asking to tag online vs. live).
- Newcomers shipping discipline features (Bankroll Buddy's stop-loss goal, Discipline Poker's self-rating) have effectively zero traction so far, so there is no market evidence yet that discipline-first positioning sells — or that it doesn't.

- Poker Bankroll Tracker's rating count is US-only; global totals unknown.

## KQ2. Category 2 — Hand-history trackers/HUDs and the grey-market converters for club apps

### Takeaway
PT4/HM3 ($99 one-time), DriveHUD 3 ($59.99) and Hand2Note 4 ($15.99–$49/mo) are all Windows-desktop products that need HH files; on club apps those files only exist if you play on a PC through an Android emulator (LDPlayer/Nox/BlueStacks) with a $25–$40 converter (KingsHands, Asian Hand Converter, Hand2Note Asia). Vendors assert "no known bans" while the platforms themselves say tracking tools are "not available," and GGPoker (ClubGG's parent network) banned all third-party software in October 2026. A mobile-only app-club player cannot use any of this.

### Cited Findings

**Desktop trackers — pricing and platform**
- WPD price list (page titled 2023, site current to 2026): Hand2Note $15.99 (monthly), DriveHUD 3 $59.99, Holdem Manager 3 $99.00, PokerTracker 4 $99.00 (14-day unrestricted trial), Poker Copilot $99.00 (macOS HUD, now also Windows), Jivaro no longer supported — [WorldPokerDeals](https://worldpokerdeals.com/soft/trackers)
- Hand2Note 4 (review updated Jun 22, 2026): Learner $19.99/mo or $15.99/mo billed yearly ($191.90), capped at $0.25bb stakes / $20+$2 MTT buy-ins and 200K-hand DB, no custom report filters; Pro $49/mo or $39/mo yearly ($468); 14-day trial; Windows 10+ with 8 GB RAM, macOS build exists; "steep learning curve"; HUD restricted on PokerStars — [PokerListings](https://www.pokerlistings.com/poker-tools/trackers/hand2note)
- Hand2Note 4 official supported-sites page: PokerBros and Pokerrrr2 supported as Android apps running in a Windows emulator (Pokerrrr2 requires LDPlayer 9); PPPoker via emulator or its desktop client; ClubGG listed under desktop clients (Windows/macOS) with its own HUD page; setup = install LDPlayer, install the poker app inside it, launch Hand2Note, HUD overlays the emulator window; GGPoker HHs can be imported for analysis but no HUD allowed — [Hand2Note 4 Help](https://hand2note4-com-test-dzf5dpdueud7cqdm.westeurope-01.azurewebsites.net/Help/supported-sites)
- PokerSnowie (NN hand evaluator, imports hands): $29.90/mo or $199.90/yr, 10-day trial, Windows/Mac; its iPhone app is rated 2.4 (17 ratings) and last updated 2018 — [AI Tools Bakery, updated Oct 16, 2026](https://aitoolsbakery.com/?p=19102)

**Grey-market converters for club apps**
- Asian Hand Converter (Ace Poker Solutions, sold via WPD): supports PokerBros, PPPoker, Upoker, Suprema, ClubGG, Pokerrrr 2, X-Poker, Red Dragon, Bingo Poker (Hitea), PokerTime, PokerKing Asia; standalone program for playing Asian apps from a PC via LDPlayer/Nox/BlueStacks; auto-locates HH folders; outputs to DriveHUD, PT4, HM3; $29 (page also says "$29/month" — ambiguous), 7-day trial; page makes no ban-risk statement; last updated 2/23/2023 — [WorldPokerDeals](https://worldpokerdeals.com/soft/hand-converters/asian-hand-converter)
- Hand2Note Asia add-on: $29, "HUD output in Chinese and club poker apps," compatible with popular Android emulators; PPPoker HUD $25 (HM/PT/H2N stats); CoinPoker HUD $60 — [WorldPokerDeals KingsHands page](https://worldpokerdeals.com/soft/hand-converters/kings-hands-club-poker-converter)
- KingsHands (operating since 2012) sells a "ClubGG Hand History Converter" that "Displays HUD during gameplay" and a GGPoker converter that "Works only with the mobile version of GGPoker"; PokerListings (updated Jul 24, 2026) states converters are "legal" on GGPoker/ClubGG/"Other Club Apps," that "The ClubGG converter is fully protected from bans," and that developers "have never received a ban complaint"; the same article says all rooms prohibit mining — [PokerListings](https://www.pokerlistings.com/blog/hand-converter-and-mining-everything-you-need-to-know) (note: affiliate content; the claims are the vendor's)
- KingsHands Chico converter (comparable product): 40 EUR/yr license locked to HardwareID, no trial; the network listed converters as prohibited software in late 2020; WPD says "no known bans" but "use at your own risk" — [WorldPokerDeals](https://worldpokerdeals.com/soft/hand-converters/kings-hands-chico-converter)

**Platform policy and ban environment**
- ClubGG (created 2021 by GGNetwork; Platinum membership $49.99/mo): "Poker tracking software such as PokerTracker or Hold'em Manager are not available," framed as keeping games soft; desktop play is via LDPlayer emulator; mobile client is based on GGNetwork's — [VIP-Grinders ClubGG review](https://www.vip-grinders.com/clubgg-review)
- GGPoker banned all third-party software (naming Jurojin, HM3, PT4) following its September 2026 superuser scandal; also announced a screen guard that blocks screenshots/screen recording of open tables and win-rate anomaly detection for RTA — [PokerNews, Oct 18, 2026](https://www.pokernews.com/news/2026/10/ggpoker-bans-huds-52622.htm)
- PPPoker policy (as summarized by a third party, page updated 2026-11-03): covers software that manipulates outcomes, offers insights "outside the player's skill," or automates play; penalties from temporary suspension to permanent ban; PPPoker "investigates before making punitive decisions"; ClubGG "bans tools that affect game integrity"; PokerBros "investigates suspicious activity before enforcing bans" — [dev.to (PokerHackORG, promotional)](https://dev.to/pokerhackorg/analyzing-penalty-policies-for-third-party-software-on-pppoker-an8)
- PokerBros (launched 2019) is described as having "a built-in hand history tracker" and being "compatible with a variety of HUD software programs"; the review does not describe export — [write.as review, Oct 23, 2023](https://write.as/ivo-manolov/the-pokerbros-application-reviewed)
- GTO Wizard's HH Analyzer lists PokerBros, PPPoker, UPoker, WePoker, PokerMaster and PokerTime among 17 supported HH formats (ClubGG is not listed) — [VIP-Grinders GTO Wizard review, Apr 2026](https://www.vip-grinders.com/?p=143094). Those formats are only produced by emulator+converter setups, so the "support" presupposes the grey-market stack above.

### Inferences
- The entire HUD/tracker stack for club apps is a PC-emulator workaround sold mostly through rakeback-agent sites (WPD) that also sell club seats. Its customers are PC-based regs, not mobile recreational players.
- Ban risk is asserted away by vendors ("fully protected") without evidence; the platforms' own language ("not available," "insights outside the player's skill") plus GGPoker's Oct 2026 blanket ban and screenshot-blocking screen guard point toward tightening, not loosening.
- A mobile-first ClubGG player therefore has literally zero compliant or non-compliant HH tooling: no file export, no desktop client without an emulator, and a parent network actively blocking screen capture.

### Gaps
- No primary ClubGG/PokerBros/PPPoker Terms-of-Service text was retrieved; policy statements above are second-hand.
- No documented ban cases (forum threads) for converter users were found; absence of evidence here is not evidence of safety.
- PT4/HM3 official current pricing pages were not fetched; $99 is from a reseller.
- KingsHands' own price list for the ClubGG/PokerBros converters was not retrieved (only comparable products' prices).

## KQ3. Category 3 — AI / solver-based hand review

### Takeaway
GTO Wizard ($39–$289/mo) owns the segment with a drag-and-drop HH analyzer gated by monthly hand caps (5 free / 100 Starter / 100K Premium); Deepfold ($19–25/mo) and Octopi ($25–65/mo) compete on coaching UX; a wave of 2025–2026 mobile AI apps (Pokr, Pokerbase AI Coach, Preflop AI, GTO Preflop Wizard, Postflop+) sells drills at $10–20/mo with tiny rating bases except for the preflop-quiz apps (3K–4K ratings). Only Pokerbase AI Coach has shipped screenshot-based hand analysis (GGPoker screenshots, Sep 2026); none supports ClubGG/PokerBros screenshots.

### Takeaway

## KQ9. Does any product combine bankroll discipline (stop-loss, cooldowns, deposit rules) with hand review? If not, why?

### Takeaway
No. Discipline features exist only as light-touch goals in two near-zero-traction trackers (Bankroll Buddy's self-set stop-loss/time limit; Discipline Poker's planned time limits and post-session self-ratings), and hand review exists only in HH-driven desktop/web products or brand-new mobile AI apps. The two sides have different customers (live recreational vs. online PC regs), different data inputs (manual ledger vs. HH files), and the platforms treat anything reading the table as a ban risk, so no incumbent has had both the data and the incentive to merge them.

### Cited Findings
- Bankroll Buddy free tier: "Discipline goals: a stop-loss or a time limit you set yourself" (no hand review); ~100 installs, 1.0★ — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6752291028)
- Discipline Poker: session planning with time limits and end-of-session notifications, post-game self-ratings correlated to results; listing does not mention stop-loss limits, cooldowns, deposit rules, or enforcement; no hand review — [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6758074740)
- Pokerbase: tracker with "Focus Mode" (unspecified) and a separate AI Coach app; no stop-loss/cooldown language in either listing — [Pokerbase](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1387987786), [Pokerbase AI Coach](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6777828462)
- Final Table combines hand logging + AI review + bankroll tracking but lists no discipline rules — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/final-table/6760188970)
- Deepfold is explicitly "not a HUD or results database; pair it with a tracker" — [Solver Scout](https://solver-scout.pages.dev/deepfold-review)
- GTO Wizard's own competitive framing is solver/trainer/analyzer, with no bankroll features in any plan description — [VIP-Grinders](https://www.vip-grinders.com/?p=143094)
- RYE sells mental game as a $597 course (Unchained Mind) separate from its $50/mo Pairrd trainer — [PokerStrategy](https://www.pokerstrategy.com/news/pokerstrategy/Save-up-to-$250-at-Raise-Your-Edge!_119979/)
- Independent reviewer argues video subscriptions produce "feeling studious without moving" — i.e., the market recognizes a behavior-change gap — [Poker Skill](https://www.pokerskill.com/blog/poker-training-sites/)

### Inferences
- Structural reasons no one has combined them: (1) trackers are live-poker ledgers whose users don't have hands to review; (2) hand-review tools are HH-file products whose users already have PT4/HM3 for results; (3) on club apps neither data source is available automatically, so a combined product would have to be built on manual/screenshot entry, which incumbents have no reason to attempt; (4) mental-game content is sold as courses, not instrumented into session data.
- The only product with both ingredients in one codebase (Final Table) is three months old with <1,000 downloads, so the combination is untested rather than disproven.

### Gaps
- No product found that enforces deposit rules or cooldowns (as opposed to self-set goals); no source discusses why.
