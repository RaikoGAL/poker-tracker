# ClubGG Terms of Service, Third-Party Software Rules, and the GGPoker "Screen Guard" (as of 2026-11-05)

Scope note: ClubGG is the club-poker app published by NSUS Ltd / NSUS Limited (App Store / Google Play developer of record); its web Terms of Service name the contracting entity as NSUS Group Inc. All dates below are explicit. Verbatim policy text is in quotation marks; anything paraphrased is labeled. Nothing here is legal advice.

Research-method caveat: the fetch tool used here caps each verbatim quote at ~125 characters and direct download of clubgg.com was blocked by the session proxy, so the ToS is reproduced as exact short fragments plus labeled paraphrase rather than full clauses. The report writer should treat quoted fragments as verbatim and everything else as paraphrase. The live ToS URL is given so the full text can be checked.

## Key Question 1: What does ClubGG's ToS/EULA say about third-party software, HUDs, trackers, screen capture, data mining, and real-time assistance?

### Takeaway
ClubGG's public Terms of Service (clubgg.com, "Last updated: June 17, 2026", NSUS Group Inc) contain NO clause that names HUDs, trackers, screen capture/screenshots, data mining, hand-history export, or "real-time assistance." The relevant restrictions are generic: a ban on "automated technology, including but not limited to robots, scripts, macros, and/or programs," a ban on using "automated technology or otherwise unfairly alter[ing] your likelihood of winning," a reverse-engineering/modification prohibition, and a rule that you may only access the service "through the interface" the company provides. Nothing in the ToS distinguishes post-session review of your own hands from real-time assistance.

### Inferences
- Because the ToS bans "automated technology ... robots, scripts, macros, and/or programs" and access "by any means other than through the interface," a tool that reads the client, scrapes tables, or injects actions would likely fall under these generic clauses; a tool that only consumes screenshots a user takes manually and is used after the session is not addressed by any clause found. This is an inference from the text, not a statement of what NSUS enforces.
- Section 19(b) incorporates "posted guidelines, policies" by reference, so NSUS could apply a GGPoker-style third-party-software policy to ClubGG without amending the June 17, 2026 ToS. No such posted ClubGG-specific policy was found.
- The ToS appears to be a generic NSUS "contest/tournament" template (references to "skill-based contests," "contest rules page," "prizes," "tickets") rather than a poker-software-specific fair-play policy.

## Key Question 2: Does GGPoker's October 2026 third-party-software ban and Screen Guard apply to ClubGG, and does ClubGG's mobile app currently allow screenshots of the hand-replay screen?

### Takeaway
GGPoker's official October 15, 2026 post never mentions ClubGG, Natural8, GGPoker.ca or GG Network skins; every secondary report found (PokerNews, Pokerfuse, PokerListings, WorldPokerDeals, TightPoker) likewise says nothing about ClubGG. However, Apple App Store version history shows ClubGG Poker v26.06.0239 (Oct 21, 2026) with the release note "Added Screen Guard" — six days after GGPoker's announcement — which is primary evidence that a Screen Guard feature shipped in ClubGG's iOS app. No source describes what ClubGG's Screen Guard blocks, whether it is on by default, or whether it covers the hand-replay/history screen. No direct user evidence (Reddit/2+2/YouTube) that ClubGG replay screenshots currently work or appear black was found.

### Inferences
- The ClubGG iOS "Added Screen Guard" note on Oct 21, 2026 is strong circumstantial evidence that NSUS ported the same Screen Guard feature from the GGPoker client to ClubGG within a week of the GGPoker announcement. If it mirrors GGPoker's implementation, it is optional and user-toggled in settings, blocks OS-level screenshots/recordings of tables, and (with a "whole client" option) may also cover history pages — but no ClubGG documentation confirms any of these details.
- The GGPoker third-party-software ban (and its Dec 1 enforcement) is documented only for "the GGPoker client." There is no evidence it has been announced for ClubGG, and ClubGG's ToS was not updated after June 17, 2026. ClubGG is primarily a mobile app, where the desktop process-detection approach described for GGPoker (closing PT4/HM3 before launch) is less applicable; the Windows ClubGG client (listed on Google Play as a platform) is the most plausible place such detection would appear, but nothing was found.
- On iOS, a Screen Guard that uses the standard secure-view mechanism would typically make screenshots of protected screens render black; whether ClubGG applies it to the hand-history/replay screen or only to live tables is unknown.

## Key Question 3: Does ClubGG have an in-app hand history / share-replay feature, and what does its own documentation say about "hand history"?

### Takeaway
ClubGG's own app-store description promises "detailed game history and statistics for games played" and statistics to "compare your skills with your friends," but uses neither the term "hand history" nor "replay." Secondhand affiliate coverage describes "hand history review," a paid "Smart HUD," and "PokerCraft" stats. No official ClubGG help documentation on hand history, hand export, or replay sharing was located.

### Cited Findings
- Official description (verbatim fragments): "Provides detailed game history and statistics for games played"; "Statistical data of the game is provided for you to compare your skills with your friends."; "Provides strong security functions and functions to prevent in-game cheating." No mention of "hand history," "replay," "export," or "share" of hands — [Apple App Store (US)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clubgg-poker/id1529839330); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nsus.clubgg&hl=en)

- "Game history and statistics" in the official description most likely refers to an in-app PokerCraft-style history/stat view (as on GGPoker), i.e., a viewable record rather than an exportable text hand-history file. No evidence of a text/export hand-history format for ClubGG was found, and GG-family products historically do not provide downloadable text hand histories — this last point is background knowledge, not sourced here.
